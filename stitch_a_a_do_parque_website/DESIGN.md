## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined gastronomic experience rooted in tactile indulgence and biological warmth. Positioned at the intersection of artisanal culinary craft and neighborhood botanical serenity in Parque Fernanda, the interface evokes the rich viscosity of pure wild-harvested açaí, the crisp structure of charred smash burgers, and the sun-dappled foliage of an urban greenhouse.

The aesthetic marries contemporary editorial layout with organic minimalism. Instead of relying on fast-food clichés, cartoon mascots, or decorative emojis, the digital presence operates like an artisanal food journal: generous negative space, intentional asymmetry, high-definition macro ingredient textures, and typography that balances historical gravitas with razor-sharp modern utility. Every touchpoint feels grounded, hospitable, and uncompromisingly premium.

## Colors

The palette draws strictly from physical matter: dense forest canopies, pulped berries, caramelization, and unbleached fiber paper.

- **Primary (`#28092B`, `#3B0E3F`, `#521558`):** Deep botanical açaí. Used for primary narrative anchors, authoritative headings, high-impact interactive states, and core brand moments.
- **Secondary (`#1B3B2B`, `#264E3A`, `#386A50`):** Verdant canopy green. Reflects the physical flora of the space, used for category indicators, dietary and origin badges, and secondary action highlights.
- **Tertiary & Culinary Accents:**
  - Ruby Strawberry (`#C92A3E`): Selective callouts, active indicators, and critical promotions.
  - Dulce de Leche / Golden Amber (`#D9822B`, `#E5A83B`): Warm craftsmanship signals, price accents, and review scores.
  - Roasted Cacao (`#422116`): Low-key structural dividers and rich footer containers.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - Canvas / Warm Cream (`#FBF9F5`, `#F5F1EB`, `#EFEAE1`): The primary atmospheric field, replacing stark sterile whites with organic warmth.
  - Surface Pure & Soft (`#FFFFFF`, `#FAF7F2`): High-clarity elevated cards and interactive layers.
- **Ink & Legibility (`#181519`, `#4A444D`):** High-contrast, warm-tinted near-blacks engineered for long-form reading, allergen disclosure, and nutritional clarity.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a rhythmic dialogue between classical gastronomy and ergonomic utility:

- **Playfair Display:** Governs the narrative pacing. Used exclusively for dish titles, collection manifestos, and experiential statements. Its high editorial contrast evokes slow-food publications and curated tasting menus.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Drives transaction, operational clarity, and interface ergonomics. Clean geometric curves with generous apertures ensure complete legibility under bright outdoor glare or dimly lit dining conditions.
- **Editorial Micro-Typo:** Category markers, origin tags, and technical specs (e.g., preparation time, botanical percentages) utilize `label-sm` rendered in uppercase with wide tracking to contrast against fluid, italicized subheads.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts reject rigid, cookie-cutter product grids. The composition uses an asymmetrical 12-column dynamic framework on desktop and a versatile 4-column column architecture on mobile.

- **Asymmetry & Editorial Pacing:** Hero items (such as the signature açaí bowl or house burger) span wide column blocks accompanied by offset floating metadata cards and bleeding ingredient imagery. Secondary accompaniments tuck neatly into staggered 2-column modules.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8px architectural cadence, punctuated by purposeful open fields (`space-xl`) that allow photography of deep purples and earthy tones to breathe without visual clutter.
- **Responsive Adaptation:**
  - *Desktop (1200px+):* 12 columns, 48px canvas margin, 24px gutters. Wide magazine-style spreads.
  - *Tablet (768px - 1199px):* 8 columns, 32px margins, 20px gutters. Hybrid vertical scroll with horizontal overflow carousels for seasonal add-ons.
  - *Mobile (< 768px):* 4 columns, 20px margins, 16px gutters. Strict single-thumb reach zone with bottom-anchored action drawers.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tactile and biological rather than synthetic. The system relies on soft warm shadows and tinted surface overlays to replicate natural daylight casting across linen and wood surfaces.

- **Ambient Light Profile:** Shadows avoid dead gray or neutral black. Ambient dispersion is tinted with deep berry and warm ochre tones (`rgba(40, 9, 43, 0.04)` to `rgba(40, 9, 43, 0.12)`), giving hovering cards an organic connection to the surface below.
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Base canvas in `#FBF9F5` or `#F5F1EB`. Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Nesting):** Interactive cards, menu modules, and ingredient groups. Background `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF7F2`, bordered with a 1px ghost boundary of `rgba(74, 68, 77, 0.08)`, and an ultra-diffused shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(40, 9, 43, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Trays & Modals):** Product detail overlays, cart sheets, and active filters. Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(40, 9, 43, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(40, 9, 43, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Fixed Overlays):** Sticky bottom order bars and global navigation headers. Supported by background blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with an 85% alpha tint of `#FBF9F5` to allow continuous sensory awareness of content moving beneath.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary balances architectural discipline with soft, organic contours:

- **Containers & Surfaces:** Medium roundedness (`0.5rem` / `8px` up to `1rem` / `16px` on larger cards) provides structure without industrial sharpness.
- **Tags & Floating Triggers:** Interactive chips, micro-badges, and floating modifier toggles transition into full pills (`9999px`) to echo the curvature of açai berries and seed pods.
- **Media Framing:** Imagery employs organic corner rounding alongside occasional asymmetrical corner treatments (e.g., top-left and bottom-right rounded at `24px`, opposing corners at `4px`) on signature hero features to establish an artisanal signature.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Açaí (`#28092B`) with crisp light cream text (`#FBF9F5`). Hover activates a subtle transition to `#3B0E3F` paired with a -1px vertical lift. Padding: 14px 28px. Font: `label-lg`.
- **Secondary Action:** Forest Green (`#1B3B2B`) ghost treatment with a 1.5px border, filling with `#1B3B2B` and white text upon hover.
- **Sensory Micro-interactions:** Sub-millimeter scale compression (`transform: scale(0.98)`) on press, providing a deliberate, tactile response.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category Filter Chips:** Neutral cream backdrop (`#EFEAE1`), dark text (`#181519`). When active: snaps to `#1B3B2B` with crisp white text.
- **Ingredient Badges:** Compact pill containers with translucent botanical backdrops (`rgba(27, 59, 43, 0.08)`) and forest green labeling (`#1B3B2B`).

### Cards & Menu Presentations
- **Editorial Dish Tile:** Avoids homogenous rectangular borders. Consists of an unconstrained high-resolution product cutout or editorial photo, overlaid with a Playfair heading, subtle provenance notes (e.g., "Açaí puro 100% nativo"), and a persistent bottom price lockup.
- **Combo Feature Modules:** Asymmetrical split surfaces pairing warm chocolate/cacao backgrounds (`#422116`) with cream typography on one side, and high-saturation culinary imagery on the other.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Warm cream interior (`#FFFFFF`), inset subtle borders (`rgba(74, 68, 77, 0.16)`). On focus: border shifts to `#28092B` with a soft tinted glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(40, 9, 43, 0.08)`).
- **Custom Quantity & Option Radios:** Custom circular selectors utilizing golden amber accents (`#D9822B`) for checked states, reinforcing natural sweetness and premium add-ons.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Customizer Accordion (The "Montagem"):** Tiered selection panel for bases, granolas, natural syrups, and fruit layers, utilizing progressive disclosure with subtle checkmarks and running price tallies.
- **Order Bar (Sticky Bottom):** Highly compact, mobile-optimized strip featuring item counts, total value in `price-display` weighting, and a unified full-width primary trigger button.
